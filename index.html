Privacy Policy — Flow Image Processor

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Flow Image Processor ("the extension", "we") is a Chrome extension that captures, processes, and organizes images from Google Flow and Instagram, and can optionally upload processed images to your Google Drive, log them in a Google Sheet you designate, and hand them to a companion WordPress extension for article insertion.

This policy explains what data the extension accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing and using the extension, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data the extension accesses

1.1 Google account data (only if you enable the Google Drive feature)

When you click "Connect Google", the extension uses Google OAuth (chrome.identity) to obtain an access token for these scopes:

ScopeWhat it allows
drive.fileCreate, view, and manage only the files the extension itself creates in your Google Drive (your processed images). It cannot see or touch your other Drive files.
spreadsheetsRead and append rows to the spreadsheet ID you enter in the extension's settings (your image queue: timestamp, article title, image number, Drive file ID/link, filename, status).

We never see or store your Google password. OAuth tokens are managed by Chrome's identity system, not by us.

1.2 Images you process

Images are processed locally in your browser (cropping, format conversion, compression, watermarks). They are uploaded to your Google Drive only if you turn on the Drive upload feature, and sent to the companion WordPress extension only if you use "Send to WordPress". Otherwise they never leave your device except via the normal download you trigger.

1.3 Instagram content (only if you use Instagram import)

When you use the Instagram import mode, the extension reads image data (image URLs and the media owner's public username) from instagram.com pages you visit, for the images you choose to import via the "+ Add" button. This requires you to be logged in to Instagram in your own browser. We do not collect your Instagram credentials or your private messages.

1.4 License information (Pro users)

If you activate a Pro license, the email address and license key you enter are sent to our licensing server solely to verify the license. Free-tier usage counters are stored locally in your browser.

1.5 Settings and preferences

Your settings (keyword, toggles, Sheet ID, article title, Drive folder ID, license state) are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage. They are never transmitted to us except as described in 1.4.

1.6 What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your browsing history, location data, financial data, health data, contacts, or the contents of your other Google Drive files. We run no analytics trackers and serve no advertising inside the extension.

2. How we use your data

  • Image processing: to convert, compress, watermark, rename, and organize the images you select.
  • Google Drive upload (optional): to store your processed images in your Drive so your publishing automation can pick them up.
  • Google Sheet queue (optional): to record one row per image (timestamp, article title, image number, Drive file ID/link, filename, status) in the spreadsheet you designate.
  • WordPress handoff (optional): to pass a processed image to the companion WP Image Inserter extension, which inserts it into your WordPress article tab.
  • Licensing: to verify Pro licenses and enforce free-tier limits.

Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the features above.

3. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The only external transmissions are:

  • Google Drive and Google Sheets APIs — data goes to your own Google account;
  • Our licensing server — license email/key verification only;
  • instagram.com / Flow web pages — only the image content you choose to capture, fetched by your own browser.

We will disclose data only if required by law.

4. Data storage, security, and retention

  • Settings and license state live in your browser's local extension storage; uninstalling the extension removes them.
  • Images uploaded to your Drive and rows written to your Sheet remain in your Google account until you delete them — we cannot delete them for you and we keep no copies on our servers.
  • We operate no database of your images, Drive contents, or Sheet contents.
  • License verification records (email + key hash) are retained on our licensing server while your license is active.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke Google access at any time: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove "Flow Image Processor".
  • Disable features: the Drive upload, Sheet queue, and WordPress handoff are all optional toggles — off by default.
  • Delete your data: remove files from your Drive, rows from your Sheet, and uninstall the extension to clear local settings.
  • Access/correction: contact us (Section 8) and we will respond to reasonable requests about the limited data we hold (license records).

6. Children's privacy

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
